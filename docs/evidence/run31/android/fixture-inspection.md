### Six direct-display recaptures

The same fixture and corrected observer `e9af6bff` reran the primary and cold-resize sequences using Android's `screencap -p` at each checkpoint. All six new PNGs were inspected. Live 2× visibly shows the enlarged heading, paragraphs and `NUWJ359`. It is byte-identical to the new cold 2× and restored-full-width 2× images. Restored 1× matches cold 1×. In all six images the two dark border rows at each edge of the input agree exactly with that stage's independently sampled native rectangle. At the narrow resized stage the nested text extends below the frame. The original stale UiAutomation PNG remains archived.
